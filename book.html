The Process

How Direct Booking Works

①
Send a Request
Pick your dates above and tap Request via WhatsApp — or just message us directly. Email works too if you prefer.
②
Quick Reply
Petar (owner) typically answers within ~30 minutes during the day to confirm availability and the exact rate.
③
Confirm with Deposit
A 50% prepayment via Revolut or direct bank transfer (IBAN) confirms the booking. No third-party processors. Balance on arrival.

Why book direct?  You skip the booking-site commission, deal with the owner directly, and we have more flexibility on early check-ins, late check-outs and small requests. Same room, same warm welcome — better rate.
